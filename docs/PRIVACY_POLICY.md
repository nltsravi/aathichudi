## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Aathichudi is built strictly **offline-first**. We do **NOT**:
- Require you to register, create an account, log in, or provide any email address or telephone number.
- Collect, transmit, harvest, or sell personally identifiable information (PII).
- Track your reading velocity, search queries, screen views, or engagement metrics.
- Utilize remote analytics tools (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Flurry, or Mixpanel).
- Employ commercial advertising networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, Meta Audience Network, or AppLovin).
- Read device identifiers (such as Advertising ID / IDFA / GAID, IMEI, MAC address, or serial numbers).
- Collect location data (GPS, cellular triangulation, or IP geolocation).

## 3. Local Storage on Your Device

All features that store user preferences or activities operate solely on your device’s private sandbox using local SQLite storage:
- **Favorites & Bookmarks**: Verses you have marked as favorites.
- **Custom Collections**: Personal study playlists, school study groups, or thematic verse collections you create.
- **Reading Progress**: Indicators showing which verses you have read and your overall completion percentage.
- **Visual & App Preferences**: Chosen visual theme (Light, Dark, or System), font size selections, and reminder schedules.

**Data Sovereignty**: This data resides exclusively in your device's isolated application storage. It is never synchronized to cloud servers, never backed up to remote databases by us, and is never accessible to third parties.

---

## 4. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Aathichudi only requests OS-level permissions when strictly required for an action you deliberately initiate:

1. **Local Scheduled Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` / `UserNotifications`)**:
   - **Purpose**: If you enable the "Daily Verse Reminder" in Settings, the app schedules a daily local alarm to display a moral verse at your specified time.
   - **Mechanism**: This runs 100% on-device through the operating system's local scheduler. No remote push server, device tokens, or network requests are used.
   - **User Control**: You can disable or change the reminder time whenever you want in the Settings tab.

2. **Photo Library / Storage (`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` / Media Storage)**:
   - **Purpose**: When you choose to export a 9:16 verse card for WhatsApp Status or social sharing and tap "Save to Photos", the app requests permission to write that single image file to your gallery.
   - **Scope**: The app never inspects, reads, uploads, or modifies your existing photo album.

3. **System Share Sheet**:
   - **Purpose**: When you tap "Share", the native system share dialog opens so you can share text or export a collection PDF to apps like WhatsApp, Messages, Mail, or Drive.
   - **Scope**: The transfer happens securely within your operating system. We do not monitor, record, or track who you share with or which target app you choose.

## 7. Data Deletion & User Rights

Since all application data is stored locally on your physical device:
- **Manual Reset**: You can clear all saved favorites, collections, and progress at any time by clearing the application data in your device's Settings menu.
- **Uninstallation**: Completely uninstalling the Aathichudi application permanently and automatically erases all local SQLite database records and stored settings from your device.
