# Privacy Policy for Aathichudi (ஆத்திச்சூடி)

**Last Updated**: October 17, 2026

Aathichudi ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This privacy policy explains the data practices of the Aathichudi mobile application available on iOS and Android.

---

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
Aathichudi is built strictly offline-first. We do **not**:
- Require user accounts, registration, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Collect, transmit, or store personally identifiable information (PII).
- Track your reading habits or transmit user analytics to any remote server.
- Employ advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral profiling tools.

---

## 2. Local Device Storage
All user interactions—including:
- Saved Favorites
- User-created Personal Collections
- Reading Progress and Last Read records
- Display and typography preferences
- Notification preference settings
are stored **exclusively on your physical device** using SQLite / local app sandbox storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share text or exported PDF files using your device's native share options.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & System Services
The application requests permissions only when an explicit user-initiated action requires it:
- **Local Notifications**: When you enable daily verse reminders in Settings, the app uses the device's local scheduled notification system. No push tokens or remote network connections are utilized.
- **Photo Library / Storage**: If you choose to save a generated verse card to your device photo gallery, access to write to your photo album is requested by the operating system.
- **Native Share Sheet**: When you share a poem or PDF, the system's standard share sheet is invoked. We do not inspect or record the destination app or recipient.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
The core application bundles all literary content locally. It does not integrate external advertising networks, cloud storage providers, or third-party tracking services.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because Aathichudi does not collect any personal data, it is completely safe for children, students, and readers of all ages in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks.
