1. Overview & Scope

Aathichudi (ஆத்திச்சூடி) ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile application created for discovering, reading, and learning the timeless verses of classical Tamil literature, including Avvaiyar's Aathichudi and Mahakavi Subramania Bharati's Puthiya Aathichudi.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy. We believe classical educational literature should be read in an environment free from surveillance, advertising interruptions, or behavioral tracking. This document describes how data is handled when you use the Aathichudi application on iOS and Android devices.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Aathichudi operates strictly on an offline-first architecture. We do not maintain any cloud database, user management service, or remote logging infrastructure.

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not require or collect your name, email address, telephone number, billing information, or social media accounts.
  • No Usage Tracking: We do not record which poems you read, how long you spend in the app, your search queries, or reading speed.
  • No Device Fingerprinting: We do not collect Advertising Identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google GAID), hardware serial numbers, IMEI, or IP geolocation.
  • No Remote Crash Reporting: The app does not transmit device state or crash dumps to external tracking servers.

3. Local Device Storage

To provide a modern, convenient reading experience, the app stores your personal usage records locally inside your operating system's private sandboxed SQLite database:

  • Bookmarked Favorites: Poems you choose to star for fast access.
  • Personal Collections: Custom groupings and study lists you build (e.g., "Morning Verses", "School Exam Prep").
  • Reading Progress: Percentage completion metrics showing your completed verses.
  • Display Settings: Your preferred theme (Light/Dark/System), font size, and notification schedule preferences.
🔒 Absolute Data Sovereignty
All of the above records stay strictly on your physical phone or tablet. They are never synchronized to our servers, never backed up to remote endpoints by us, and are not accessible to any third-party app.

4. Device Permissions Explained

Aathichudi asks for device permissions solely to support explicit features that you activate:

  • Local Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / UserNotifications):
    When used: When you activate the "Daily Verse Reminder" in Settings.
    How it works: The notification is scheduled locally by the operating system’s timer. No remote push server, push tokens, or internet connections are used. You can disable this anytime in Settings.
  • Photo Gallery / Storage (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription):
    When used: Only when you tap "Save to Photos" on the 9:16 verse card generator.
    How it works: Permission is used exclusively to write the newly generated card image to your gallery. The app does not read, inspect, or modify existing photos in your library.
  • System Share Dialog:
    When used: When you tap "Share" on a poem or PDF collection.
    How it works: Handled entirely by the iOS or Android native share sheet. We do not see or record the recipient or destination application.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Aathichudi does not integrate commercial SDKs or third-party web services:

  • No Ad Networks: No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, or Meta Audience Network.
  • No Analytics: No Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, Segment, or Facebook SDK.
  • No Cloud Sync: All literature and explanations are stored locally in the app bundle.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Family Friendly)

Aathichudi is an educational tool suitable for students, children, and families of all ages (PEGI 3 / Everyone / 4+).

We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international children's privacy regulations. Because we do not collect any personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where applicable). The app does not offer public chat rooms, external hyperlinks to uncontrolled web domains, or user accounts.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store your data on external servers, you have total control over your local data at all times:

  • Reset within App: You can clear your favorites, collections, and progress directly through the app interface.
  • System App Storage Clear: On Android, you can go to Settings > Apps > Aathichudi > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstallation: Removing the app from your device immediately and permanently deletes all local databases and preference files.
